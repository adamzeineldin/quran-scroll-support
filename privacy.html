Privacy Policy

Quran Scroll for iPhone

Quran Scroll does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, reading position, settings, and the Ayah of the Day widget's display choice are saved only on your iPhone. They are removed when you delete the app.

When the app goes online

The app connects to the internet only to download a recitation the first time you play it. The audio comes from the Islamic Network audio service (cdn.islamic.network). That request contains nothing about you beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address. Downloaded recitations are kept on your device so later plays work offline.

Notifications

If you turn on the Ayah of the Day notification, it is scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved and no notification token is collected.

Study notes

Study notes are written ahead of time and bundled with the app. No AI service is contacted while you use it, and nothing you read or search is sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
